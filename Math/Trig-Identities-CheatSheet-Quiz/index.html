How to Use

Color Coding

Red = Sine (y-axis) | Blue = Cosine (x-axis) | Purple = Tangent (sin/cos)

Pink = Cosecant (1/sin) | Light Blue = Secant (1/cos) | Light Purple = Cotangent (1/tan)

On the unit circle: x = cos θ and y = sin θ

Cheatsheet Mode

Check identities to study. Selected items show details by default (click to collapse).

Presets: Use the dropdown to quickly load curated sets:

  • 🔧 Engineering Essentials - Core identities for engineering students
  • 📚 Beginner Trig - Fundamental identities and basic concepts
  • 📖 Intermediate Trig - Sum/difference formulas and double angles
  • 🎓 Advanced Trig - Half angles and product/sum conversions
  • ∫ Calculus Focus - Identities commonly used in calculus
  • 📐 Geometry & Triangles - Laws and area formulas

Quiz Mode

Toggle Quiz Mode from the cheatsheet page to hide each formula until you tap an identity to reveal it.

Desktop Controls:

  • Click the Quiz Mode button to turn the feature on/off
  • Click any identity to reveal its formula
  • Use the Screensaver button for the auto-advancing slideshow
