GETTING STARTED

A flexible way to review Calc 1 derivatives.

01

Build a study set

Use checkboxes to choose formulas. The study-set menu selects a whole topic at once. Your selection is saved in this browser.

02

Practice recall

Turn on “Practice: hide formulas” to quiz yourself in the list, or open Flashcards to reveal one formula at a time.

03

Review later

Save tricky formulas from a card or flashcard. Open Saved to remove items or export your list as TXT or CSV.

04

Color cues

Sine and cosecant use warm red; cosine and secant use blue. The same cues appear throughout the app.

Included so far: the Calc 1 derivative rules provided for this app. More course topics can be added to this same formula library.
