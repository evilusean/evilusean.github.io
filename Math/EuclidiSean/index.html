Euclid's Elements

How to Use

This screensaver cycles through propositions and definitions from Euclid's Elements, animating each geometric construction step by step.

  • Play / Pause — toggle automatic advancement.
  • Previous / Next — step through entries manually.
  • Randomize — jump to a random step.
  • Book filters — check or uncheck books to include in rotation.

Move the mouse to reveal controls. They fade away during passive viewing.
